Dear Siri ❤️

A STORY THAT STARTED ON AUGUST 6, 2020

August 6, 2020 (Sravani Marriage lo😻)

Ee date naaku eppatiki gurthundipothundi. Endhukante aa roju ninnu first time chusinappudu, nijanga em jarigindo naaku appudu ardham kaaledhu. Nuvvu chala cute ga unnav. Ninnu chusina aa moment nunchi, endhuko teliyadhu, naa attention motham nee meedhane undipoyindhi. Aa roju motham ninnu admire chesthu unna. Next day kuda adhe feeling. Appudu naaku oka question vachindhi... "Idhi attraction aa? Leka love aa?" Naaku appudu aa feelings ki peru pettadam kuda raadhu. Aa roje neeku cheppeddham anukunna. Kaani appudu nenu 8th or 9th class chaduvuthunna. Ela cheppalo teliyadhu. Chepthe em anukuntavo ani bhayam. Andhuke appudu naa feelings ni naalone dachukunna. Kaani appudu naaku teliyadhu, aa chinna feeling oka roju six years journey avuthundhani.

August 6, 2021 – August 6, 2026

Chusthu chusthune six years aipoyayi. Ee six years lo enno sarlu neeku cheppali anukunna. Kaani prathisari edho oka reason tho aagipoya. Konni feelings cheppakunda unte marchipotham anukuntaam. Kaani konni feelings matram time tho paatu inka special aipothayi. Naa vishayam lo kuda adhe jarigindhi.

Our Little Memories ❤️

Manam eppudo oka marriage lo kalavadam, festivals lo kalavadam, appudappudu maatladukovadam... Bayataki chusthe avi chala normal moments la anipinchachu. Kaani naaku matram avi chala special memories. Neetho maatladina prathi sari, neetho konchem time spend chesina prathi sari, aa moments ni nenu chala special ga feel avuthanu. Nuvvu naatho spend chesina time neeku oka normal memory ayyi undochu. Kaani naaku matram avi naa life lo gurthundipoye memories.

Teja Pelli, Shekharma Pelli & Shankar Pelli

Ee weddings lo neetho spend chesina konni moments naaku ippatiki gurthunnayi. Especially Shankar pelli roju... Around 2 o'clock time lo nee room lo unna moments gurthunnayi. Nuvvu saree lo chala beautiful ga unnav. Nijam cheppalante, aa roju ninnu chusi em compliment ivvalo kuda naaku ardham kaaledhu. "Chala baagunnav" ani cheppali anukunna. Neetho inka konchem maatladali anukunna. Oka photo kuda adugudham anukunna. Kaani akkada andaroo undatam valla adagalekapoya. Next day aina maatladudham anukunna. Kaani nenu vere panullo busy aipoya, nuvvu kuda tondaraga vellipoyav. Ala aa opportunity kuda miss aipoyindhi. Ippudu venakki thirigi chusthe, aa chinna moments kuda entha special ga anipisthunnayo cheppalenu.

Something I Never Told You...

Nijam cheppalante, neeku chala compliments cheppali anukunna. Nee smile gurinchi, nee beauty gurinchi, nee dressing gurinchi, nee way of talking gurinchi... Kaani cheppaledhu. Endhukante nuvvu vaatini ela teesukuntavo naaku teliyadhu. Nenu edhaina compliment isthe, nuvvu wrong ga understand chesukuntavemo ani bhayam. Direct ga hint ichina, nannu tease chestharemo ani anipinchedhi. Annitikanna ekkuvaga, mana madhya unna relationship spoil aipothundhemo ani bhayapadda. Andhuke chala sarlu cheppali anukunna feelings ni kuda navvuthu cover chesesa. Kaani okati matram cheppali... Nuvvu beautiful ga unnav ani cheppadaniki nee appearance okkate reason kaadhu. Nuvvu maatlade vidhanam, nee smile, nee presence... ivanni kalipi ninnu naaku special ga anipinchela chesthayi. Neetho maatladinappudu time ela gadichipothundo kuda teliyadhu.

Why Am I Telling You Now?

Six years nunchi naa mind lo unna oka feeling ni inka dachukovadam naaku ishtam ledhu. Repu em avuthundho naaku teliyadhu. Mana future ela untundho kuda teliyadhu. Kaani oka vishayam matram naaku clear ga undhi. Neeku cheppakunda unte, idhi naa life lo oka regret ga migilipothundhi. Andhuke ee roju neeku chepthunna. Siri, naaku nuvvante istam. Aa istam eppudu start ayyindho exact ga cheppagalanu. August 6, 2020. Kaani aa feeling ni ela explain cheyyalo matram ippatiki nerchukuntunna. Nenu ninnu six years nunchi admire chesthunna ani cheppadam kanna, ee six years lo nuvvu naaku entha special ga anipinchavo cheppalanukuntunna. Nenu perfect person ni ani cheppanu. Naa daggara anni answers unnayani kuda cheppanu. Kaani ninnu respect chesthu, nee feelings ki value isthu, genuine ga undagalanu.

One Last Thing... ❤️

Nenu ee story chepthunnadhi nee nunchi immediate ga oka answer expect chesthu kaadhu. Nuvvu em feel avuthunnavo, nee mind lo em undho, adhi naaku important. Neeku kuda naa meedha alanti feelings unte, nijanga adhi naaku chala happy moment avuthundhi. Neeku konchem time kavali anipisthe, adhi kuda completely okay. Leka neeku alanti feelings levu anipisthe, adhi kuda nenu respect chesthanu. Endhukante naa feelings cheppadam naa responsibility. Kaani nee feelings decide cheyyadam nee right.
